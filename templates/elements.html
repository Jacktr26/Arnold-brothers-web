About Us

Here's a little bit about who we are, what we do, and where we're headed.

Fin and Dec — aka the Arnold brothers — are from Chester, England, but with a strong Irish heritage behind them. Aged 16 and 19, they’ve already played Devafest Festival on the same day as Ocean Colour Scene, Toyah Wilcox, Sleeper and more — performing just before breakout Britain’s Got Talent star Olly Pearson.

They’ve built up a solid following playing bars and pubs around Chester and local festivals — including the now legendary “Westfest” underground folk and rock celebration — and have supported local charities and sustainability events such as Chester Zoo’s Wildlife Connections Festival. They’ve even played the odd bar in Dublin.

Their musical influences range from The Kooks, Foo Fighters, Radiohead, Arctic Monkeys, and Catfish and the Bottlemen to Wunderhorse, Kingfishr, and Loyle Carner.

They’re currently in the studio recording their first E.P and loving every minute of it. If you want to hire them for a party, event, or fly them to Vegas to headline a casino residency for a few months, please reach out and get in touch. Their rider is very reasonable and stacked mainly with Moams, Cola Bottles, and possibly the odd tray of nachos and beer.
