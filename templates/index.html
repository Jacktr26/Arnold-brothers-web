Live Performances

Two guitars, one pulse. Fin and Dec blend clean chime and analog grit to deliver an indie-rock sound that feels live, loud and real. We’ve played festivals, pubs, school events and private parties around Chester and beyond.

We can keep it stripped-back and chilled, or push it louder for a proper gig atmosphere. If you’re running an event and want live guitar, we’re up for it.

Original Music

As well as covers, we write and play our own tracks — indie guitar tunes with melody, riffs and a bit of grit. Our originals are starting to take up more space in our sets as we shape our sound.

We’re working on recording and releasing our first tracks. Keep an eye on this site and our socials for demos, live clips and future releases.
